# Employee learning roadmap: Mini-CA + CFO + Family Office

12-month plan for the CA practice employee. Original: [learning-roadmap.pdf](learning-roadmap.pdf). Filed 2026-11-01. No start date set yet.

## Goal

Not a traditional practising CA. The employee becomes the person who understands a client's whole financial operating system, diagnoses problems, coordinates specialists (CA/CS/legal partners do the licensed work), and later runs the firm and its tech.

Target market: SMEs plus HNIs (actors, athletes, doctors, senior tech people).

## Syllabus: 70 modules in 9 groups

| Group | Modules | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| A. Accounting & financial control | 1-9 | Bookkeeping, bank rec, month-end close, statements, MIS, Tally |
| B. Indian tax & compliance | 10-18 | GST ops, TDS, income tax, capital gains, notices |
| C. Government documentation | 19-24 | Registrations, MCA/ROC, portals, DSC, compliance calendar |
| D. Fundability & funding | 25-34 | Credit analysis, 0-100 fundability score, CMA, loans, schemes |
| E. Finance / virtual CFO | 35-43 | Cash flow, working capital, budgeting, 3-statement model, FP&A |
| F. HNI / promoter finance | 44-50 | Personal tax, ESOP/RSU, succession, family office concepts |
| G. Client management | 51-56 | Onboarding, 60-90 min health check, pricing, proposals |
| H. Firm operations | 57-63 | SOPs, maker-checker QC, data security, regulatory boundaries |
| I. Business building | 64-70 | Sales, HNI acquisition, partner network, firm scaling |

Most are marked Critical. Full table in the PDF.

## Phases

Learner → Practitioner → Financial Controller → CFO → Firm Builder → Platform Owner

| When | Focus | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Months 0-3 (Phase 0) | Accounting, GST, TDS, income tax, statements, cash flow, working capital, portals, modelling, funding, HNI finance, sales. Build dummy client files while learning | Understands a client's full financial picture. Passes the graduation test |
| Months 3-6 | 5-10 real or pilot clients. Time: 70% client work, 20% learning, 10% business building | For every client: books → compliance → statements → MIS → cash flow → health score → funding readiness → recommendations |
| Months 6-12 | FP&A, virtual CFO, fundability, MIS dashboards, business advisory | Firm sells "we become the finance function of your business" |

## Key tests

- **Financial diagnosis (the #1 exam):** fictional ₹25 Cr revenue company with full data (P&L, BS, cash flow, GST, TDS, bank, receivables, payables, loans, inventory, tax history). "Tell me what is wrong with this business": profit vs cash, working capital, debt, GST rec, receivables risk, margins, can it raise ₹5 Cr, what to fix first, next month's actions.
- **Phase 0 graduation test:** a Client Financial Health Report on a hypothetical SME, 10 parts: snapshot, compliance health, financial health, cash flow, fundability score /100, gap report, 90-day plan, funding requirement, funding package, CFO recommendations.

## HNI vertical (possible second business line)

Personal CFO / Family Office Lite: income architecture, tax, investments, ESOP/RSU, liquidity, net worth, debt, insurance, asset ownership, estate planning, family finances, major purchases, personal dashboard, tax calendar, professional network.

## End state: 10 capabilities

Accounting, tax, compliance, financial analysis, cash flow, fundability, CFO, HNI finance, client management, firm building.
