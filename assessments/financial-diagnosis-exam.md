# Financial diagnosis exam ("the #1 exam")

**Setup:** a fictional company with ₹25 Cr revenue and its full data set: P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, GST data, TDS data, bank statements, receivables, payables, loans, inventory, tax history. (Data set to be shared later.)

**Question:** "Tell me what is wrong with this business."

**He should identify:**
- [ ] Why profit isn't becoming cash
- [ ] Whether working capital is excessive
- [ ] Whether debt is sustainable
- [ ] Whether GST reconciliation has issues
- [ ] Whether receivables are dangerous
- [ ] Whether margins are deteriorating
- [ ] Whether the company can raise ₹5 Cr
- [ ] What needs fixing before approaching a lender
- [ ] What management should do next month
